The 4 Phases

The MDS classifies the macro environment into one of four regimes based on two axes: Growth and Inflation. Each axis is either accelerating or decelerating.

PhaseGrowthInflationNicknameBest Sectors & Assets
P1 AcceleratingDeceleratingGoldilocks Technology, Consumer Discretionary, Comms, Financials · Growth equities, Bonds, USD

A Transitional state is shown when signals are mixed. In that case: reduce concentration, diversify, and wait for clearer signals.

How Phases Are Determined

Each indicator is tagged as a Growth or Inflation measure. The system computes breadth — the weighted percentage of indicators on each axis that are accelerating.

  • Breadth > 55% = Accelerating
  • Breadth < 45% = Decelerating
  • 45–55% = Transitional

US core indicators carry 2.0x weight (Unemployment, CPI, Core CPI, Payrolls, Retail Sales, Industrial Production). Leading indicators carry 1.5x (Housing, Surveys, Jobless Claims, Durable Goods).

Indicator Scoring (-5 to +5)

Each indicator receives a composite score from three components, all normalized by the indicator's own volatility:

  • 3-Month Acceleration Trend (1.5x) — Smoothed momentum of the year-over-year trend
  • Latest Monthly Acceleration (1.0x) — Most recent month's change
  • Trend Position (0.5x) — Latest YoY vs. 12-month moving average

Score Color Guide

+3.0 or higher — Strongly Bullish
+1.5 to +3.0 — Moderately Bullish
+0.5 to +1.5 — Slightly Bullish
-0.5 to +0.5 — Neutral
-0.5 to -1.5 — Slightly Bearish
-1.5 to -3.0 — Moderately Bearish
-3.0 or lower — Strongly Bearish

Quarterly Regime (QoQ SAAR & YoY)

The panel under the stats bar adds NIPA/BLS hard data — Real GDP, Real PCE, Real GDI, Employment Cost Index, Core PCE — on two horizons: an annualized quarter-over-quarter run-rate (SAAR) and a year-over-year rate, plus whether the run-rate is accelerating. These are blended into the Phase breadth at their weights — a quarterly series counts as accelerating when its SAAR run-rate rises vs the prior quarter.

Regime Conviction (Fractal Geometry)

Ported from the Fractal Box Analysis Tool: the Hurst exponent, box-counting fractal dimension, and trend R² of the YoY trajectories combine into a 0–100 conviction score. It doesn't pick the phase — it tells you how much to trust it. A persistent, clean trajectory (high Hurst, low fractal dimension) = high conviction; a choppy, mean-reverting one = low conviction / elevated transition risk.

Reading the Dashboard

  • Check the Phase — The stats bar shows which of the 4 phases the economy is in
  • Check Conviction — Regime Conviction tells you how stable/trustworthy that phase is
  • Review Breadth — Growth and Inflation breadth percentages show the balance of accelerating vs. decelerating indicators
  • Scan the Scores — Green rows are bullish, red rows are bearish. Sort by score to find strongest/weakest signals
  • Check the Calendar — Upcoming data releases that could shift the picture
  • Position Accordingly — Use the phase-based sector and asset guidance in the table above
